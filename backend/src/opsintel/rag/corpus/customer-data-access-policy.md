# Customer data access policy (restricted)

Customer names, emails and phone numbers are personal data. Access is limited to admins with
a support ticket reference. Incident responders work with customer IDs only. Bulk export of
customer records requires approval from the data protection officer. Break-glass access is
logged and reviewed weekly.
